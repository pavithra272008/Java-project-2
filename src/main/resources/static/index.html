Conflict Detection & Business Rules Playground

Interactive testing suite demonstrating the core business rules specified in Use Case #44.

1

Zero Overlap Invariant (Conflict Detection)

No two confirmed bookings for the same room may overlap in time. The service layer verifies newStart < existingEnd && newEnd > existingStart before saving, immediately throwing HTTP 409 Conflict.

2

Instant Slot Reclamation on Cancellation

When an employee cancels a booking, its status transitions to CANCELLED, freeing the slot immediately for other teams to book without conflict.

3

10-Minute No-Show Grace Period & Auto-Release

Organizers must check in within 10 minutes of meeting start. A background daemon (running every 30s) automatically detects unconfirmed no-shows and releases the room back to public inventory.
